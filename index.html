About CivCoin

CivCoin is a crypto-backed movement to make governments transparent, fair, and accountable. Australians deserve to know where every tax dollar goes — and have a say in how it’s spent. This isn’t just a coin — it’s a call for power to return to the people.

Our Vision

Imagine a world where paying tax earns you rewards. Where voting on budgets is easy as scrolling TikTok. Where government waste gets exposed, and your voice helps shape the country. That’s the future CivCoin is building.
