About CivCoin

CivCoin is a new digital currency aimed at empowering citizens. By rewarding engagement and transparency, CivCoin enables people to participate directly in decision-making processes and government spending.

How It Works

Earn CivCoins by taking civic actions, voting on proposals, and supporting projects. Use your tokens to vote, influence budgets, and receive rewards for your involvement.

  • See where your tax dollars go
  • Have a say in spending
  • Earn real rewards for civic action
